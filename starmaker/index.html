How You Play

Run Your Own Agency

Your headquarters is the heart of everything. Move between the recording studio, the staff office, the practice room and the lounge — manage your capital, check the market, hold briefings, and push the calendar forward one week at a time.

Weekly Turns Bank & Capital Market Briefings Mail & Items

Scout & Train Your Idols

Spend gold to scout new faces and read their potential at a glance. Every trainee has six stats and an overall grade — some are born performers, others need years of practice before they're ready for the stage.

Vocal Dance Diligence Creativity Stamina Charm

Build Groups That Debut

Assemble your trainees into groups of any size — a duo, a trio, a full lineup. Take them from pre-debut through their first release and watch their buzz climb month after month as the fanbase grows.

Every group builds its own discography. Pick the tracks you want on rotation and listen to them right inside the game with a full player — shuffle, skip, and repeat your agency's greatest hits while you work.
