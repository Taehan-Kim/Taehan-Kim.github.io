Sorcerer's Note
Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 25, 2026  ·  BLADE SOFTWARE LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how BLADE SOFTWARE LLC ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use Sorcerer's Note ("the App"), an iPad handwriting and note-recording application.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

Sorcerer's Note is designed to work locally on your device. We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the App:

  • Notes and handwriting data — stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or access your note content.
  • Audio recordings — stored locally on your device only.
  • Exported video files — generated and saved on your device. We do not retain copies.
  • Basic app diagnostics — anonymous crash reports used solely to improve stability (no personally identifiable information).

2. Google Account & YouTube Integration

We do not store, retain, or share your Google account credentials or YouTube data.

When you choose to upload a session video to YouTube, the App uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 authentication flow. Your Google account login is handled entirely by Google's secure authentication system — we never see, store, or transmit your Google username, password, or tokens outside of the authenticated upload session.

YouTube upload data (video files and metadata you explicitly submit) is sent directly to YouTube's API on your behalf. Once the upload is complete, no YouTube data is retained by the App or our servers. We access only the minimum YouTube API scopes required to perform the upload you initiate.

  • We do not read, store, or analyze your YouTube channel data, subscriptions, or history.
  • We do not retain Google OAuth tokens beyond the active session.
  • You can revoke the App's YouTube access at any time via your Google Account permissions page.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information described above solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the core features of Sorcerer's Note
  • To perform YouTube uploads that you explicitly initiate
  • To diagnose crashes and improve App stability

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage & Security

All notes, recordings, and exported videos are stored locally on your iPad using iOS standard file storage. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect any data handled by the App:

  • All API communications use HTTPS / TLS encryption
  • No note content or recordings are transmitted to our servers
  • OAuth tokens are used only in-memory and are not persisted to disk

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • YouTube Data API v3 (Google LLC) — used only when you choose to upload a video. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store — subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Since Sorcerer's Note stores data locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time by removing the App or clearing its data in iOS Settings.

For any privacy-related requests, contact us at admin@bladesoft.io.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
