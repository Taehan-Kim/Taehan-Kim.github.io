Sorcerer's Note

The most natural way to capture your thoughts on iPad — with the full power of pen, voice, and video, all in one place.

iPad App

Write, Record,
and Share

Sorcerer's Note combines free-form handwriting, PDF annotation, audio recording, and video export into one seamless experience — designed from the ground up for iPad.

  • ✏️ Free-form Pen Notes
    Write, sketch, and annotate freely with your Apple Pencil — just like paper, but smarter.
  • 📄 PDF Annotation
    Import any PDF and write directly on top of it. Mark up documents, textbooks, or contracts with your own handwriting.
  • 🎙️ Record & Write Simultaneously
    Start a recording and take notes at the same time. Every stroke is time-synced to the audio so nothing gets lost.
  • ▶️ Replay Your Sessions
    Play back your entire note-taking session as a video — see every pen stroke re-drawn in sync with the audio, exactly as it happened.
  • 📤 Share & Upload to YouTube
    Export your session replay as a video and upload it to YouTube to create study materials, lecture recordings, or tutorials.
